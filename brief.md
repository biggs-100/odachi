## Decisions taken
- The three adoptions are the gaps the comparison found, not the whole gentle-ui-design.
- The numbers stay decisive: their refusal to give a scale is not adopted, because an agent without one invents.
- The figures cite their source and their limit: WCAG 2.2 gives 24x24 as the criterion, and a ratio is not conformance.
- There is no sensitive data in the first version.
- First usable version: today.
- Wants to learn: whether a state with its copy written first stops the generic error the tells cannot catch.
