# a-feature-records

## What must exist
- a feature records that its review is waived, and the gate says so

## Done when
- the task doc carries the waiver, and only when the human asked for it
- a closed feature with a waiver is ready, and check names the waiver instead of a verdict
- progress reports the waiver as its own state, and the contract test pins the name

## Not included (first version)
- the per-tree exemption the close already has
- review mode off and its meaning
- a waiver that hides the proof or the fence
- the panel itself

## Decisions taken
- It is used from the computer.
- There is no sensitive data in the first version.
- First usable version: today.
- Wants to learn: how a recorded exception stays honest.
