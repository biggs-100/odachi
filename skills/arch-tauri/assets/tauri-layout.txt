## Constraint: capabilities/ and permissions/ stay in the app crate

`tauri-build` resolves both globs from the APP CRATE directory, not the workspace root:

- `tauri-build-2.6.2/src/acl.rs` calls `parse_capabilities("./capabilities/**/*")` (~line 428)
  and `env::current_dir()?.join("permissions")` (~line 308).

A workspace split is safe as long as the app crate remains the crate that (i) runs
`tauri_build::build()` in its own `build.rs`, (ii) hosts `generate_handler!`, and
(iii) contains `capabilities/**/*` and `permissions/**/*`. Moving those two
folders to the workspace root breaks the ACL build.

UNCONFIRMED: whether tauri-cli v2 mis-discovers the built binary when a workspace
hoists `target/` to the workspace root.
