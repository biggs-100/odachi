# From a draft to a document that can be shown

The value is not that a model wrote the draft. It is that a person can answer:
which version was reviewed, and with what evidence.

## The five moves

1. **Write the intent before the draft.** What must the document answer, and what
   must it not contain? One paragraph in the requester's own words. Do not
   rewrite that sentence later; it is what the result is judged against.
2. **One claim, one source.** For every figure, date, rule or quotation, record
   the primary source, the exact excerpt and the day it was checked. A secondary
   summary is not the source.
3. **Verify, then write.** Check the source before the claim enters the document.
   If it cannot be checked, say so in the document instead of guessing.
4. **Review with authority.** A named person reads the result and can stop,
   correct or invalidate it. Record who reviewed which version and what changed.
5. **Keep the trail.** Draft, source list, checked excerpts and review note travel
   with the final version. A document without its trail is an assertion.

## How this maps to odachi

- The **brief** is the intent paragraph: what has to exist and what is excluded.
- A **requirement** is the requester's sentence quoted verbatim, not a paraphrase.
- **Proof** is the evidence tied to one document version. In odachi today the
  gate reads the output of a command; for document work the "command" is the
  concrete verification step the person runs and records, for example opening
  the official text and copying the exact article. The tool does not yet accept
  a non-command proof, and this reference does not pretend it does.
- A **review** is the independent pass with a named finding, not a stamp.

## What to say when the person asks for shortcuts

- "Just make it sound official": the document must say what was checked, not
  sound as if it was.
- "The AI said the norm says...": name the norm and article from the legal map,
  and check the official text before repeating it.
- "Nobody will audit this": a public document can be read by any citizen; the
  trail is what makes an answer possible later.

## Stop conditions

Stop and ask when the document would decide about a person, would carry reserved
or personal data, or would state a legal conclusion. Those are not drafting
choices; they belong to the entity's officer and legal area.
