# Peru AI legal map for office work

Orientation verified 2026-11-23 against official texts. This is not legal advice.
Consult your entity's Oficial de Inteligencia Artificial and legal area for a
decision that binds the institution. Full research with source links:
`docs/office-peru-research.md`.

## Norms in force

| Norm | Date | What it changes for a document worker |
|---|---|---|
| Ley 31814, promoting AI use | Published 2023-07-05 | Sets principles and names PCM-SGTD as authority. Detailed duties are in the regulation. |
| Reglamento, DS 115-2025-PCM | Published 2025-09-09; most clauses effective 90 business days later | Prohibited and high-risk uses, transparency, human oversight, entity duties. |
| ENIA 2026-2030, RM 152-2026-PCM | Signed 2026-04-29, published 2026-05-01 | Mandatory three-year AI action plan, OIA designation, quarterly reporting, disciplinary exposure. |
| Directiva 002-2026-PCM/SGTD | Published 2026-10-21 | OIA profile and safeguards for third-party AI services. |
| Ley 29733 + DS 016-2024-JUS | 2011; regulation published 2024-11-30 | Personal data rights, consent, breach notice, data protection officer, security. |
| ANPD guidance on data in AI systems | 2026-11-11 | Official orientation, not a sanction. |

The authority is the same body under two names: ANPD in gob.pe, ANPDP in the
regulation. No AI-specific ANPD sanction and no Peru-specific Contraloría guide
for public servants were found; do not cite one.

## The boundary that matters at a desk

- **Prohibited uses** include deceptive manipulation, mass surveillance without legal basis, and biometric inference of race, politics, religion or sexuality.
- **High-risk uses** include choosing or dismissing workers, granting benefits, credit, health access and emotion inference at work. These are not drafting tasks.
- **Acceptable uses** such as drafting and summarising still require human review, privacy and transparency.
- Public-facing AI content needs a label. Internal drafts are exempt only when they do not affect rights or services.
- Every entity needs an institutional AI policy and an OIA. Ask for both before changing how a report is produced.

## Do and do not

| Do not | Do |
|---|---|
| Paste personal, reserved or sensitive data into a public tool. | Minimise and anonymise before any AI-assisted processing. |
| Present AI output as verified fact. | Verify each figure and citation against the primary source and record it. |
| Copy third-party text or images without rights clearance. | Keep a human reviewer able to stop, correct or invalidate the output. |
| Assume an internal draft needs no label. | Label public-facing content and keep the review evidence. |

## Limits of this map

The exact entry-into-force date of DS 016-2024-JUS is reported as 2025-03-31 by
secondary sources and was not recalculated. Congressional debate about amending
Ley 31814 is not law. No enacted amendment to the law or its regulation was found.
